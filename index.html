How to read this

Each card shows its own age. Current means the information was confirmed in the last week. Needs a refresh or Out of date means nobody has confirmed it recently, so treat it as old rather than as wrong.

If a course looks stuck

Write to Tal Feder, the programme head, and say which course and what you need. A card that stays out of date is a problem on our side, not something for you to work around.

This page is not Moodle

Assignments, files and submissions all stay in Moodle. This page answers only where each course has got to, so you do not have to open every course to find out.
